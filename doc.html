    Collect what tailoring turned up

    While you tailor a resume, the LLM asks recall questions and your answers raise old claims and uncover new ones. It lists them at the end as the master resume feed. Copy that block from the conversation.

    A unit whose ID already exists is replaced. A new unit goes to the end of its stack. Anything that is not a unit (a new JD vocabulary mapping, say) is left for you to add by hand.
