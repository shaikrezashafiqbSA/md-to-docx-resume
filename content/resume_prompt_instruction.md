Line rules:

Role line: always a level-3 heading with exactly 3 fields separated by " | ", in this order: contracted role title, employer, dates. The tool prints it on one line, title first and boldest, employer second, dates at the right margin. Write it the same way for every role. Bold text, or commas between the 3 fields, breaks it.
Role title: the contracted title only. Join two titles held at one employer with a comma (Quantitative Developer, AI Engineer). Team, department, division and programme names go in the scope note.
Employer: the official name of the organisation, written once, with no department.
Dates: "Mon YYYY to Mon YYYY" or "Mon YYYY to Present". Three-letter month, the word "to". Education and certifications carry the year only.
Role line length: 85 characters at most, counted from the first letter of the title, so it holds one line in the docx. Over 85, shorten the title and move the detail to the scope note.
Scope note: the first paragraph under the role line, plain text. The tool styles it.
Bullets: "- " only, one level deep. No blank line between consecutive bullets. One blank line between every other block.
Bold: only the label that opens a bullet, written **Label:** with the colon inside the bold. Bold appears nowhere else.
Leave out: italics, tables, horizontal rules, level-4 headings, nested bullets, emoji, HTML, and text outside the block.
Pipe: appears only in the contact line, role lines and education lines.
Links: [display text](url), with the link text and URLs exactly as specified. Write every URL as display text.
Section headings: the 7 above, in that order, in capitals, as level-2 headings. Add, rename or merge none.
